LEARNING YEAR-ROUND

AG Academy

Continue building Academic Games skills beyond the summer camp experience.

Keep learning beyond camp.

AG Academy is Camp AG’s online learning platform designed to help students continue learning and practicing Academic Games beyond the summer camp experience.

Through lessons, explanations, and practice activities, AG Academy gives students a place to review Academic Games concepts, strengthen their skills, and keep learning at their own pace throughout the year.

  • Visit AG Academy

Continue Learning Beyond Camp

Camp AG provides an in-person summer experience where students learn and play Academic Games with other campers and student instructors. AG Academy extends that learning online, making it possible for students to keep practicing and learning throughout the year.
